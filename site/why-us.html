03 — Radical transparency

Transparency the industry hasn’t seen.

Most agencies ask for your trust. We’d rather earn it in the open. Five pledges we make to every client, on every project.

  1. 01

    You see every draft.

    Not just the final cut. Every edit, every version, every option — shared as we go, so nothing that goes live is ever a surprise.

  2. 02

    Itemized quotes. No hidden extras.

    Every line priced before we begin. If the scope changes, you’ll hear it from us first — never for the first time on an invoice.

  3. 03

    Honest advice, even when it’s inconvenient.

    If a listing isn’t ready — the photos, the timing, the story — we’ll tell you so. Our name is on the work too.

  4. 04

    Real reporting. Good numbers or bad.

    Plain-language reporting on what’s working and what isn’t. No vanity metrics, no spin, no burying a slow week.

  5. 05

    Your assets are yours.

    The imagery, films and files we create for your listing are delivered to you, organized and ready to use wherever you need them.

04 — Privacy & exclusivity

Same aircraft. Same topic. Never the same work.

We care about your privacy. When two projects share an aircraft type or a topic, they still never share ideas, footage or strategy. Every client gets work made only for them.

  1. Article I

    Confidential by default.

    Your aircraft, your client, your numbers. Discretion isn’t an upgrade — it’s how every project starts, from the very first call.

  2. Article II

    Never recycled.

    We don’t double-dip. Your concepts, footage and strategy are never reused in another client’s project — even when the aircraft or the topic is the same.

  3. Article III

    Always our first.

    Our visionaries and creatives bring every skill they have to your business — with the same care as the very first project we ever made.
