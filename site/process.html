    A deep-dive on your aircraft, your buyers and your goals — so we agree on what a win looks like before anything else moves.

    One focused session on the aircraft (or the one you're after), the market you're selling into and the people who sign. We audit what already exists, so nothing useful gets made twice.

    Leg 02

    Plan

    A custom strategy and creative direction, mapped to the channels that actually move aircraft.

    What happens
    We turn the brief into a flight plan: the story, the look, the deliverables and the channels — adverts, spec sheets, web and social — sequenced so every piece lands when it should.
    What you get
    A written plan you can read in minutes, with every deliverable and date in plain sight.

  3. 03
    Leg 03

    Capture

    On-location shoots, air-to-air, drone, studio and video — built for high-impact storytelling.

    What happens
    Our crew plans every frame around the aircraft's best angles and the light that flatters them, then captures stills, film and detail — on the ramp, in the hangar or in the air.
    What you get
    A complete library of imagery and footage: the raw material for every asset that follows.
    Your part
    Access to the aircraft. We coordinate the rest.
  4. 04
    Leg 04

    Produce

    Edit, design and develop. Polished assets, ready for every touchpoint.

    What happens
    Retouching, colour grading, editing, design and build — brochures, spec sheets, films and listing sites. You see it once, finished, before it goes live — not a string of drafts.
    What you get
    One finished, consistent set of assets: a clear message across every page a buyer touches.
    Your part
    One look before launch.
  5. 05
    Leg 05

    Launch

    We deploy, distribute and measure — so every next move is sharper than the last.

    What happens
    Your listing goes live where buyers actually browse — adverts, listing sites, social and spec sheets — and we watch what performs from day one.
    What you get
    A live campaign and plain-language reporting on what's working, and what we'd change.
    Your part
    Take the calls. That's the point.

Questions

Before you board.

Straight answers to what brokers ask us first. Anything else, ask us on the call — or write to hello@uppersky.media.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. No fee, no catch, no invoice afterwards. It's a real conversation about your sale or acquisition strategy, and you leave with an honest recommendation either way.

Do I commit to anything?

No. The consultation carries no commitment at all. If we're the right fit, we'll put a clear plan in front of you — whether you go ahead is entirely your call.

How fast can we launch?

We get you up and running in days, not weeks. Exact timing depends on your aircraft, your goals and what's already in place — we'll map it out together in the consultation, with every date in plain sight.

Do you work with single brokers?

Absolutely. We work with industry professionals and single brokers alike. Whether you run a brokerage or you're working one deal, you get the same standard, the same transparency and one point of contact.

Who owns the content?

You'll know before we start. Ownership and usage rights are agreed in writing up front, so there's no fine print waiting for you later. Bring any questions to the consultation.

What do you need from me?

Less than you'd think. Access to the aircraft (or the details of the one you're after) and a few decisions at the key moments. We handle the rest — so you can stay focused on your buyers and relationships.
