Before you ask

Good questions.

Straight answers on what the consultation is — and what it isn't.

The quickest answer is usually a conversation.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first conversation costs nothing — no fee, no card, no catch. It's how you find out what we'd actually do for your deal, and how we find out whether we're the right fit.

Do I commit to anything by booking?

No. You leave with an honest read on your sale or acquisition and, if you want one, a plan with a transparent, itemized quote. Whether you take it any further is entirely up to you.

What should I prepare?

Nothing formal. It helps to know the aircraft — make, model and year if you have them — where you are in the deal, and your timeline.

Existing photos, a spec sheet or a live listing are welcome, but optional. Just come ready to talk about the goal.

Do you work with single brokers?

Absolutely. We work with single brokers and established industry professionals alike. Whether you're representing one aircraft or a full book of listings, you get the same team and the same standard — and once we agree on a plan, we're up and running in days, not weeks.

What happens to my details?

They're used for one thing: replying to you. We never share them and never sell them, and everything you tell us about your deal stays between us.
